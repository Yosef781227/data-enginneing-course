Motion

  • Check pop: green circle scales 0.3 → 1, 500 ms, overshoot (cubic-bezier .2,1.6,.4,1)
  • Rise: new feedback slides up 10 px and fades in, 450 ms
  • Progress fill: bars and rings grow from 0, 900 ms ease-out
  • Wrong gap: small shake, 350 ms, never red flashing
  • Turn all of it off when the system asks for reduced motion

Words

  • Sentences under 15 words. One idea each.
  • Explain a new term the first time, in “New words”.
  • Buttons say what happens: “Check my answer”, not “Submit”.
  • Mistakes are normal: “Not quite”, never “Wrong!”.
  • Always show time: “About 8 min”.

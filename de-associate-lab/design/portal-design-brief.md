Please design a web app called **DE Lab**: a personal study app for the Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate exam.

**Who it's for:** a complete beginner with no data-engineering background, whose first language may not be English.
They get lost in long technical text. They learn best with simple words, everyday analogies, pictures, and doing things themselves.

**What the app contains:**

1. **Hands-on labs** (like guided labs on Udemy or Cloud Academy)
   - 9 labs, one for each exam topic, about 40 steps in total.
   - In each step the learner:
     - reads a short lesson: an analogy, a simple diagram, slides from their course, a plain explanation
     - fills in numbered gaps in some code, runs it and sees the output in a built-in terminal
     - presses "Check": if it's right, the step gets a green checkmark and the next step unlocks
     - answers one exam-style question
   - A "show solution" option is there for when they're stuck.

2. **Question practice**
   - 280 practice questions, grouped by the 7 official exam sections and 26 concepts.
   - After answering, the learner sees: the idea being tested, why the right answer is right, why each wrong option is wrong, and a warning when the source answer is doubtful.
   - Missed questions come back later for review (spaced repetition).

3. **Mock exam**
   - A timed 45-question exam (90 minutes), split like the real exam.
   - No hints during the exam; a results breakdown by section at the end.

4. **Progress**
   - Progress per lab, per exam section and overall; weak topics; what to do next.

**What we want to achieve:**
- It always feels clear what to do next, without any confusion.
- Learning feels encouraging and rewarding, with satisfying progress and small animations.
- The look is friendly, calm and modern, and pleasant to spend hours in.
- Lab and practice feel like one app, not separate tools.

Please design all the screens you think this needs: the layout, navigation, style and colours that work best.
